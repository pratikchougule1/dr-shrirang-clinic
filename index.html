About the Consultant

Dr. Shrirang Yadwadkar is a dedicated Consultant Laparoscopic & Colorectal Surgeon specializing in advanced minimally invasive (keyhole) surgeries. With a Diploma in Minimal Access Surgery (Hernia) and Fellowship in Colorectal Surgery, he focuses on painless recovery, precision techniques, and compassionate patient-centric care at Madhur Laparoscopy Clinic.
