About the Consultant

Dr. Shrirang Yadwadkar is a Gold Medalist Consultant General Laparoscopic & Laser Surgeon, specializing in Hernia, Colorectal, and Robotic Surgery at Madhur Laparoscopy Clinic, Mulund (West), Mumbai. Dedicated to "Healing Wounds Without Scars," he offers minimally invasive keyhole and laser treatments for rapid, painless recovery.
